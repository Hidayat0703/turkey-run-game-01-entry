Gobble Run — The Great Turkey Escape: a side-scrolling chase game where you are the turkey, the farmer and his net are right behind you, and 900 metres of farmyard, harvest lane and farmhouse kitchen — fences, ovens, falling carving knives and all — stand between you and the Freedom Woods. Hurry and hide: sprinting burns stamina, ducking into a hay bale makes his search-the-bale AI lose your trail, and three lost feathers means Thanksgiving is back on.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. No build step, no network: serve `game/` with any static server. Sound is synthesized live with WebAudio.

Screenshots are deterministic scenes from the game's own code: `?shot=title|farm|kitchen|end&seed=7`.
